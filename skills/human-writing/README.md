# human-writing: what I think it is

A short guide for website copy, product text, social posts and emails, so they sound like a real shop owner and not like a chatbot.

What's inside: a list of tell-tale AI words ("delve", "seamless", "elevate"...), sentence habits ("not only X but also Y", everything in threes, endings like "..., ensuring quality"), and what to do instead: concrete facts, prices, opening hours and the customer's own words.

My take: these are signs, not proof, since people use these phrases too. The point is clearer and more specific copy, not tricking AI detectors.
